How to Play

  1. You start with 150 flowers.
  2. Pick a flower from the Flowers tab.
  3. Tap the circle to lay it down.
  4. Drag flowers to arrange them; tap one to select it.
  5. Use Symmetry for instant patterns.
  6. Switch on Sketch if you have an idea in mind.
  7. Hit Turn Sketch into Pookalam to fill your drawing.
  8. Use all 150 for Full Bloom.
  9. Save and share your creation.
